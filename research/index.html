Research & patents

Patents and papers on retrieval-augmented generation, AI security and knowledge graphs.

  • System and Method for Human Presence Verified Access Control of Retrieval-Augmented Generation Data2026
    Indian patent application 202641076803, published July 2026, filed through VIT.
    In plain words: human-presence patent

    An AI assistant can read private documents only while a real person is proven to be present, so malware on a fully compromised computer can’t quietly pull the data out.

  • System and Method for Preventing Large Language Model Prompt Injection and Identity Spoofing2026
    Indian patent application 202641022703, filed through VIT.
    In plain words: prompt-injection patent

    Stops attackers from hiding instructions in what an AI model reads, or posing as someone else, to make the model do things it shouldn’t.

  • CT-KG-RAG: A Threat Knowledge Graph Framework for Graph-Structural Intervention Simulation and Hypothetical Attack-Path Reasoning2026
    Peer-reviewed IEEE conference paper.
    In plain words: threat knowledge graph paper

    Maps how cyberattacks connect as a graph, then lets an AI ask “what if”: which attack paths open or close when one part of a system changes.

  • Clarifying the Role of Non-Linear Models in Financial Markets and Stock ForecastingUnder review
    Non-linear forecasting models explained with SHAP and LIME.
    In plain words: stock forecasting paper

    Tests whether complex models really predict Indian stock prices better than simple ones, and shows why they make the predictions they do.
